# Last Bell v4 external approval gates

No item in this file is complete merely because a frontend or contract feature
exists. Named owners and dated evidence are required before mainnet.

## Independent audit

- Solidity round, manager, lifetime vault, reward router, payment router, and
  production swap adapter reviewed.
- Quote API, keeper, indexer/reorg logic, deployment scripts, and reward
  reconciliation reviewed.
- Privileged roles, change boundaries, and operational conversion reviewed.
- Every finding has disposition, remediation commit, regression evidence, and
  auditor confirmation.

## Legal and eligibility

Counsel must provide an approved matrix covering:

- Supported and blocked jurisdictions.
- Age, identity, and sanctions requirements.
- Stock Token and USDG eligibility restrictions.
- Gaming, gambling, sweepstakes, and financial-promotion treatment.
- Persistent lifetime rewards and recurring future-pot participation.
- Required risk, automation, custody, price, and tax disclosures.
- Privacy, retention, access, deletion, and incident-notice obligations.

Until that matrix exists, value-bearing availability is fail closed. Frontend
controls are not represented as preventing direct permissionless contract use.

## Safes and operational ownership

- Three common owner addresses supplied and independently controlled.
- Three distinct audited 2-of-3 Safe addresses deployed and verified.
- Manager admin, market/catalog admin, and treasury assigned.
- Keeper and conversion keys isolated, encrypted, narrowly authorized, and
  minimally funded.
- Named incident, treasury, keeper, security, legal, and communication owners.

## External-wallet and production services

- Production Reown project ID and exact testnet/production origins.
- MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase, Safe, WalletConnect QR, and EIP-6963 UAT.
- Desktop/mobile paid flows, all failures, allowance editing/reuse, every reward
  category, batch claims, and transaction replacement.
- Monitoring, alerts, on-call ownership, treasury procedure, and public incident
  channel proven.

## Authorization

- Separate written authorization for the two-round mock testnet smoke.
- Separate written authorization for the exact mainnet broadcast commit.
- Separate approval of the clean 24-hour unlisted canary report before public
  promotion.
